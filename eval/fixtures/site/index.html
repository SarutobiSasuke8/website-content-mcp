Fixture Bakery

Fixture Bakery bakes sourdough bread every morning in a synthetic town. This page exists only so the contract suite has a stable, local page to read.

Opening hours

We open at seven in the morning and close when the sourdough sells out. Orders for whole loaves can be placed a day ahead.

Every loaf is proved for at least sixteen hours, which gives the crust its colour and the crumb its open texture.
